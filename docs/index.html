Argue

Rehearse the conversation you keep avoiding — and keep a record of what you actually said.

it does not take sidesThere is no score and no winner. The pushback exists to make you say it better, not to beat you.
you
I need the weekends to be shared, not assumed to be mine.
them

pushback
That answer invites a fight about the past. Try what you want next week, not what they should have known.
you
Fair. From next week, can we alternate Saturday mornings?

Rehearsal, not victory

The pushback is there to find the sentence that starts a fight before you say it to a person. It is not a debate you can win.

What it keeps

Your positions and the pushback you got, so you can read back what you actually said rather than what you remember saying.
