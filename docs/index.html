Argue

Practise being pushed back on. One half is a counterparty with a hidden goal and a script; the other is the strongest honest case against your own position, with a record of how your confidence moved.

Open Argue
Status Built and working offline: three bundled scenario packs (US student visa, campus placement, rent negotiation) with a hidden goal each; difficulty 1–3, where 2 and 3 push back on your previous answer instead of moving on; a scorecard that quotes a sentence you actually said, names an opening you did not take, counts filler words and rewrites your weakest answer; and a steelman with a confidence changelog.
Release: Windows EXE, Android APK and Linux binary, built from this source in CI — app-latest.
Not built: the adaptive AI counterparty. The Rehearsal prompt asks for an LLM that improvises; there is no model or key here, so the counterparty is a bundled script and a rule — and the app says so rather than pretending a model is in the room.

What it does

  • Puts you across from a named counterparty whose goal is hidden from the room: to find out whether you intend to come back, to fill the role at the lowest CTC, to hold the rent at his floor.
  • Asks the pack's questions, and at difficulty 2 and 3 pushes back on your last answer, quoting a word you used.
  • Scores the session from the transcript only: a sentence you actually said, an opening you missed, filler words counted, your weakest answer rewritten.
  • Builds the strongest honest case against a claim, from seven bundled argument moves.
  • Records your confidence before and after, and keeps a changelog of how your mind moved.

What it will not do

  • Pretend a model is in the room. The counterparty is a script; the counter-case is a bank of moves. Both say so.
  • Score an empty turn. Say nothing and nothing is scored — and the scorecard refuses to run on no answers.
  • Count a restatement as opposition. A “counter-case” that repeats your own words is flagged as a restatement.
  • Invent a grade. Every line of the scorecard is counted from your transcript; the rewrite is a filler-removal rule, not a model.
  • Use a key or a network. No fetch, no endpoint; CSP connect-src 'none'.

When things go wrong

CaseWhat happens
Bad inputA rehearsal with no stated counterpart is refused — practising against nobody teaches nothing. An empty turn is refused rather than scored. A position with no claim is refused.
No networkNothing changes: the packs, the moves and the transcript are on the device.
Storage full or unavailableThe session continues in memory and the app says the transcript will not be kept.
Corrupt saved stateReported and offered for export. The app never opens a blank session in its place, which would look like a lost rehearsal.

How to run it

  • Here: play/index.html, or install it from your browser's menu.
  • Offline file: open docs/play/index.html straight from disk.
  • Tests: node tests/self-tests.js — the spec's countable “done means” is asserted there.
